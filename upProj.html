up - Storytelling Music Video

View an excerpt of the video on the left. See the whole video here.


Up is a short storytelling music video, created as part of a college solo assignment.


The brief for this project was to create a short film cut to music, that tells a story visually, under the theme of 'connections'. The emphasis was on composition, editing and visual storytelling skills.


Since this was a solo project, I was responsible for all elements of the final video. This includes shooting video, editing and storytelling.


The story I tried to tell with this video is one about the connection between humans and technology, and how they evolve side by side. I did this through editing footage that I had taken, both on a DLSR and my phone, with images and footage from the website Internet Archive to the song 'Up' by Oneohtrix Point Never.


My main focus was on the edit. I kept the pace fast, cutting between shots along with the music, and layered images on top of eachother to show connection and create a surreal image. One special technique I used to create interesting imagery was using an interpolation software to blend through a folder of old images to create a rapid morphing blending effect. Another was editing the whole thing, playing it through a projector onto a wall and recording that to add a whole other layer of texture and colour.

Tools

  • PremierePro - video editing
  • Phone camera and DLSR - photos and videography
  • Projector - projecting and re-recording video
  • Internet Archive - sourcing archive footage and images

Skills

  • Cinematography - composition and filming
  • Editing - laying out exhbition and interaction design
  • Creative Vision - ideation and execution

I am really proud of how this video turned out. I think the creative filmaking and editing makes it stand out, and I think the audio and visuals work really well in tandem to tell the non-linear, abstract story that I wanted to tell.
